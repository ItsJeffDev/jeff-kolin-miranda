my journey

How I got here

From the first line of code to shipping real products — the milestones that shaped the way I build today.

2023

The first line of code

Started with HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, and basic Python out of pure curiosity. Small static pages, broken layouts, and a lot of late nights followed. I wasn't always consistent, and there were plenty of breaks along the way, but I kept coming back to coding. Each time, I learned something new and continued building from where I left off.

2024

Growing as a Developer

I kept learning and upskilling, even when my progress wasn't always consistent. Every project, challenge, and return to coding gave me something new to learn, gradually improving my skills and helping me grow as a developer.

2025

Client projects

Delivered production websites for clients — a flower shop storefront and a front-end replica of the OSHC site. Learned that deadlines, feedback, and handover matter as much as clean code.

2025

Best in Research

Built OOPSphere with my prompting skills, an interactive platform that makes Object-Oriented Programming easier to learn. The team earned the "Best in Research" award at our defense presentation.

2026

Freelancing & building bigger

Now working as a freelance full-stack developer — designing databases, shipping platforms like SakayMoto, and writing software I'd be comfortable handing to another engineer.

next

What's next

Deepening my software engineering fundamentals, taking on larger systems, and looking for a team where I can grow fast and contribute from day one.
